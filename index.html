Biography

Dr. Zhihao Xuis working at the UBINET Key Lab, Qingdao University, China. His research interests include artificial intelligence with applications, explainable machine learning, and intelligent transportation systems. He is the associate editor of the Review of Artificial Intelligence and the Chinese Journal of Computer Science. Besides, he is the editorial member of Information Processing & Management, a guest editor of Future Transportation, a program Committee member of ISAIR 2022, and a reviewer of Innovation, IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, ACM Transactions on Knowledge Discovery from Data, ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD), IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, Information Fusion, Transportation Research Part C, Transportation Research Part E, Knowledge-Based Systems, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Chaos, Solitons and Fractals, Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, Journal of Transport Geography, Results in Engineering, Swarm and Evolutionary Computation, Expert Systems with Applications, Information Processing and Management, Computers & Industrial Engineering, Future Generation Computer Systems, Applied Soft Computing, Neural Networks, Neurocomputing, Complex & Intelligent Systems, Decision Support Systems, Scientific Reports, Applied Intelligence, Physica A: Statistical Mechanics and its Applications, Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory, BMC Public Health, The Journal of Supercomputing, Journal of Systems Architecture, Water Resources Management, Data & Knowledge Engineering, Journal of the Franklin Institute, International Journal of Climatology, International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction, Earth Science Informatics, Transportation Research Interdisciplinary Perspectives, Frontiers in Public Health, Frontiers In Sustainable Cities, Sensors, Applied Sciences, Mathematics, Electronics, Heliyon, Automatika, International Journal of Information Technology & Decision Making, Future Internet, International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction, Systems and Soft Computing, International Journal of Intelligent Networks, Machine Learning with Applications, Environment and Social Psychology, and so on

Academic Services

I serve as Associate Editor of Review of Artificial Intelligence, Editorial Board Member of Information Processing & Management (CAS Zone 1), and Lead Guest Editor of the Special Issue on Low-Altitude Transportation and Air-Ground Collaboration in Future Transportation. I am also a member of the Program Committee for the international academic conference ISAIR 2022.


In addition, I act as a reviewer for more than 30 high-impact journals and conferences, including Innovation (IF=25.7, Cell Press), IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, ACM TKDD, and KDD. To date, I have reviewed over 300 academic papers.
